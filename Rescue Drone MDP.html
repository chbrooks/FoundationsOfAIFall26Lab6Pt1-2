Rescue Drone MDP

A drone launches from base to reach a stranded hiker. Every move uses battery, gusty canyon cells use double, and wind sometimes pushes the drone sideways. Landing on a charging pad refills the battery. If the battery runs out anywhere else, the drone crashes. The state is position and battery, so the same square can call for different moves.

The first tab plans with a full model of the wind and battery. The second takes the model away: the drone has to learn from its own flights with Q-learning.

Try

Wind pushes the drone 90° left or right of its intended direction, each with half the slip chance. Flying into a cliff or the edge of the map leaves it in place but still uses battery. Changing any setting restarts every algorithm on both tabs.

Policy iteration

Evaluate solves linear equations for the exact value of the current policy. Improve switches every state to its best action. Outlined cells switched in the last improvement.

Q-learning

Here the drone does not know the wind chances or where a move leads. It only sees what happens on flights it actually makes. After every move it nudges one number, Q(state, move), toward what it just saw: Q ← Q + α [ r + γ · max Q(next) − Q ]. With enough flights and enough exploring, these numbers approach the values the Planning tab computes from the model. A flight ends at the hiker, at a crash, or after 60 moves.

α, ε and the start rule change on the fly, so you can raise ε partway through training. Changing the starting Q-values or the seed starts learning over. The same seed always gives the same flights.

Things to try

  • No exploring. Set ε to 0, reset, and add 3,000 flights. The drone keeps repeating whatever worked first, and the learned policy stays well below optimal. Reset with ε at 0.2 and do the same: it gets close to optimal. (Early on, both curves bounce around; compare them after a few thousand flights.)
  • Where it never goes. With flights starting at base, open the Visits view at battery 7. Most of the map is grey: the drone can't be far from base with a full battery unless it recharged. Switch to random starts and compare.
  • Learning rate and wind. Try α = 0.5 with the default wind. Each gust swings the Q-values, so the policy keeps changing. Then try it with wind slip 0.
  • Learning vs. doing. In the progress chart, training flights still crash sometimes even after the learned policy is good. That is the cost of exploring with ε.
